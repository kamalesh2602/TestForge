# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone who participates in the TestForge community.

We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, nationality, personal identity, or technical ability.

We welcome contributions from people with different perspectives and skill levels, and we value constructive collaboration.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward other contributors.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Providing constructive and helpful feedback.
- Accepting different opinions and approaches.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or insulting language.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberate intimidation or threats.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

They may take appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, issues, and other interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Discussions and code reviews.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Any other public or private space where someone represents the TestForge project.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers through GitHub.

Please contact the repository owner privately rather than creating a public issue for sensitive reports.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated appropriately, with respect for the privacy and safety of those involved.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports and determine appropriate responses based on the circumstances.

Possible actions include:

1. A warning and request to stop the inappropriate behavior.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation in project spaces.
3. Removal of contributions or comments.
4. Temporary or permanent exclusion from the project community.

Maintainers are expected to communicate enforcement decisions clearly and fairly.
